Where this comes from. Each entry is a call the police logged, not a confirmed conviction. Locations are rounded to the block.

Fraud and scam reports are usually filed from the victim's home, so they mark where people live more than where crime happens. Shopping areas like Chestnut Hill and Needham Street show more theft because that's where the stores are.

If you share this with clients as a realtor, present it as public police data and let them draw their own conclusions. Fair housing rules discourage steering buyers toward or away from neighborhoods.
